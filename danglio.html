Questions

Will the charm block my clicks?

No. Only the charm itself responds to your mouse. Clicks anywhere else on your screen go straight to the app underneath.

Will it slow my computer down?

Danglio stops drawing as soon as the charm is still, so it uses almost no processing power while resting. It wakes for a breeze or when you touch it.

Can I hide it during a presentation?

Yes. Press Ctrl+Alt+H, or use the tray icon, to hide and show the charm at any time.

What happens to my purchases if I change computers?

Sign in with your email on the new computer, or enter the order number from your receipt under Restore purchases. Your packs unlock again.

Do I need an account?

No. Danglio works fully without one. An account only lets your purchases follow you to your other computers, and you can delete it from Settings at any time.

When is the Mac version coming?

Windows comes first. The macOS version is being built alongside it and will follow. Leave your email below to hear when each is ready.
