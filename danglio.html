Opening Danglio on a Mac for the first time

  1. Open the downloaded Danglio.dmg and drag Danglio into Applications.
  2. Open Danglio from Applications. macOS says it can't check the app; click Done (or OK).
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Danglio, then confirm with your password.
  4. That's it. From now on Danglio opens normally.

Becoming an Android tester

  1. Join our tester group: groups.google.com/g/danglio-testers (sign in with the Google account you use on your phone, then click Join group).
  2. Accept the test on Google Play: play.google.com/apps/testing/com.installngo.danglio, then click Become a tester.
  3. Install Danglio from Google Play: Danglio on Google Play. It can take a few minutes to appear after step 2.
  4. Please keep it installed for at least 14 days, and tell us what you think at installngo.official@gmail.com.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Danglio free?

Yes. The app and a set of charms are free. Extra charms, packs and Danglio Pro are optional one-time purchases.

Where can I get it?

Windows: from the Microsoft Store. Mac: download it here. Android: it's in testing on Google Play, and you can join as a tester.

Why does my Mac say it can't check Danglio?

The Mac download isn't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. It's safe to open: follow the four steps above once, and it opens normally after that.

Will it slow my computer down?

No. Danglio sleeps whenever the charm is still and uses almost no power or memory.

Will it get in the way of my work?

No. Clicks pass straight through to whatever is underneath; only the charm itself responds. You can hide it any time from the tray or with a keyboard shortcut.

Does it show during movies and games?

By default it fades away when a video, game or slideshow goes full screen, and comes back afterwards.

Do I need an account?

Only to buy. Signing in with your email lets your purchases follow you to up to five computers.

Can I get a refund?

If something you bought doesn't unlock, or you were charged twice, we refund it in full. See our Cancellation and Refund Policy.
