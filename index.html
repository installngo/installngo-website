How we work

Three commitments that apply to every product we release.

Private by default

We collect only what a product needs to work. No names, serial numbers or IP addresses, and you can switch anonymous statistics off.

Made for many languages

Our software follows your system language and can be changed in a click, starting with English, Tamil, Hindi and Malay.

Kept up to date, safely

Updates are checked for our signature before they install, and never touch your settings or purchases.
