Questions

Good questions, short answers

Is Danglio free?

Yes. Danglio and its charms are free right now, with no ads.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (Microsoft Store) and Mac (macOS 11 or newer). Android is in testing on Google Play, and you can join as a tester.

Why does my Mac ask before opening it?

The Mac download isn't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. It's safe: follow the four quick steps once, and it opens normally after that.

Will it slow my computer down?

No. Danglio sleeps whenever the charm is still and uses almost no power or memory.

Will it get in the way of my work?

No. Clicks pass straight through to whatever is underneath; only the charm responds. Hide it any time from the tray or with a keyboard shortcut.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it, pick a charm and it hangs right away.

How do I get help?

Email installngo.official@gmail.com. A real person replies, usually within two working days.
