What is Danglio?

Danglio is a free desktop app for Windows and Mac by installngo. It hangs a lucky charm from the top of your screen that you can pull and tap, and its Desk buddy walks in to remind you about water, breaks and meetings.

Is Danglio free?

Yes. Free charms, free packs and Desk buddy reminders, with no ads. Danglio Pro is one payment, for life, for every premium charm and pack, Create with AI, the Charm timer and everything new.

How do I add a Desk buddy reminder?

Press Ctrl Alt B (Cmd Option B on Mac) from any app, write what to remind you about, pick the time and the days, and press Enter. You can also open Danglio → Desk buddy → Your reminders.

Will the buddy walk in during my meeting or presentation?

No. It waits while a full-screen app or slideshow is open. To keep the buddy and the charm out of screen sharing, turn on Hide from screenshots and screen sharing in Settings, or pause the buddy from the tray.

How does Create with AI work?

Pick a style in Danglio and copy its prompt. Paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini with your photo, save the picture it makes, and upload it in Danglio. Danglio removes the background on your computer and hangs it as your own charm.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

Not by Danglio. The background is removed on your computer and your photo charm is kept only there. You choose what to share with ChatGPT or Gemini, under their terms.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 from the Microsoft Store, and Mac (macOS 13 or newer) as a direct download. Android is in testing on Google Play, and anyone can join as a tester.

Will it slow my computer down?

No. Danglio sleeps whenever the charm is still and uses almost no power or memory. Clicks pass straight through to whatever is underneath.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it, pick a charm and it hangs right away. Sign in with your email only if you buy, or want your purchases on all your devices.
