Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Danglio app and installngo.com collect, why, and your choices. In short: we collect very little, we never sell it, and we show no ads.

1. Who is responsible

installngo is a brand of [LEGAL NAME], a sole proprietor based in [COUNTRY] ("installngo", "we", "us"). Contact: installngo.official@gmail.com.

2. What the app collects

  • Anonymous install information. At most once a day, Danglio sends a random install ID (created on your computer, not linked to you), the app version, your operating system and your language setting. We use it to count installs and to see which versions and languages are in use. From the language setting we derive a country (for example, Tamil in India becomes India). We do not store names, serial numbers, hardware IDs or IP addresses with it. You can switch it off in Settings → Privacy, and nothing is sent after that.
  • Purchases. When you buy a pack, we store the order number, which pack you bought, the amount and currency, the date, and the install ID or account it belongs to, so the app can unlock your charms and restore them later.
  • Accounts (optional). If you create an account, we store your email address and the list of computers you use Danglio on (install ID, app version, operating system and when each was last seen).
  • Settings. Your charm, size, position and other preferences are stored only on your computer.

3. Payments

Payments are handled by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects and processes your payment details, name, email and billing address under its own Privacy Policy. We never see or store your card details.

4. The website

installngo.com uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics. Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site securely. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and the files for downloadable charms. Like any server, it handles technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver the service.
  • Paddle: payments, invoices, tax and refunds.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.

They process data only to provide their service to us. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

6. How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept for as long as you may need to restore your purchases, and as required for tax and accounting. Account information is kept until you delete your account. Install information for computers that stop using Danglio is deleted or anonymised after [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 24 months].

7. Your choices and rights

  • Switch off anonymous install information in Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete your account at any time in Settings → Account. This removes your email address and unlinks your computers. Purchases stay with the computer that paid for them.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing installngo.official@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

8. Children

Danglio is a general-audience app. Purchases and accounts are meant for adults, or for children with a parent's permission. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and announce important changes in the app or on this website.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: installngo.official@gmail.com.
