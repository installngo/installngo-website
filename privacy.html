Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Danglio for Windows and Android works without an account. It keeps an anonymous id for each device and, unless you switch it off, sends the app version, the system (Windows or Android) and the language setting once a day. If you choose to sign in, we get your email, and the name and country you give us. Your wallpaper photo never leaves your phone. We don't show ads, sell data, track you across apps, or collect IP addresses in the app.

We never collect: your phone number, location, contacts, IP address, hardware serial numbers, files, photos, screenshots, what you type, or which other apps you use. Your settings, favourites and chosen charm stay on your device.

Your wallpaper photo (Android): when you choose a photo for behind the charm, Android lets Danglio see only that one photo. Danglio keeps a copy inside the app on your phone to draw the wallpaper. 3. Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Razorpay may ask for your phone number and email and collects your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive the payment id, amount, status and the contact details Razorpay shares with the payment, and never your full card number, bank password or UPI PIN.

4. This website

installngo.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It's hosted by Vercel, whose servers briefly log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the site secure. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it sends them.

5. Why we're allowed to use it

We use your email, name and country with your consent, which you give by signing in and filling them in. We use purchase records to deliver what you paid for and to meet legal duties such as tax and accounting. The anonymous install info is used only for the statistics above, and you can stop it in Settings.

6. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal data. We share it only with the service providers that run Danglio for us, only as needed:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage;
  • Razorpay: payments;
  • an email delivery service: to send sign-in codes;
  • Microsoft: distributes the Windows app through the Microsoft Store under its own privacy statement;
  • Google: distributes the Android app through Google Play under its own privacy policy;
  • Vercel: hosts this website.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside India. We choose providers with strong security, and we'll share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Email, name, country and linked devices: until you delete your account.
  • Install info: while the app is in use, and for up to 24 months after the device was last seen.
  • Purchase records: as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (up to 8 years). After you delete your account they stay linked only to the anonymous install id, not to your email.
  • Messages to us: up to 2 years after your issue is closed.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct or update it, or erase it, and you can withdraw your consent. You can also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you.

  • Delete your account yourself: in the app (Windows: Settings → Account; Android: More → Account) choose Delete my account. We confirm it with a code sent to your email, then remove your email address, name and country and unlink all your devices.
  • Stop install info: switch off Share anonymous install info (Windows: Settings → Privacy; Android: More → Privacy).
  • Delete without the app, or anything else: email installngo.official@gmail.com from the address on your account.

If you're not happy with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

You can use Danglio's free charms without giving us any personal data. Accounts and purchases are for people aged 18 or older; a parent or guardian must sign in and buy for anyone younger. If you think a child has given us their email without that, please contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The app holds no keys to our database; only our server functions can read account and purchase data, and admin access is limited to named people. Charm lists are digitally signed so the app can tell they really came from us.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes we'll tell you in the app or by email before they apply.
