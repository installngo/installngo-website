4. Why we use it

  • To provide the Services, including sign-in and syncing purchases across your devices (on the basis of your consent, given when you sign in).
  • To deliver purchases, process refunds and keep records required by tax and accounting law (to fulfil the purchase you requested, and to comply with law).
  • To greet you by name and to understand, in aggregate, which countries, languages and versions to support (on the basis of your consent).
  • To respond to your requests and protect the Services against misuse.

You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 9). This does not affect processing already carried out, or records we must keep by law.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that process it on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage;
  • Razorpay: payment processing;
  • Resend: delivery of sign-in emails;
  • Microsoft: app distribution through the Microsoft Store, under its own privacy statement;
  • Vercel: hosting of this website.

We disclose data to authorities only where required by law. Some providers store data outside India, as permitted under the DPDP Act.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to it is restricted, and purchase records sent to the apps are digitally signed. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable safeguards appropriate to the data.

8. Children

Accounts and purchases are for people aged 18 or older, or a parent or guardian acting for a child. Free features can be used without providing personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may access a summary of your personal data, correct or update it, have it erased, withdraw consent, nominate a person to exercise your rights, and seek grievance redressal. You can:

  • edit your name and country, or delete your account, in the app;
  • switch off device data in the app's privacy settings;
  • email installngo.official@gmail.com for any other request, from the address linked to your account.

We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. This website

installngo.com uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. Our hosting provider records technical data, such as IP addresses, for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version; material changes will be notified in the app or by email.
