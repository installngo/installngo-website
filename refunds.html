Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy applies to all purchases made in installngo apps and on installngo websites. All our products are digital and are delivered immediately after payment.

1. Full refund

You are entitled to a full refund if, within 7 days of payment, you tell us that:

  • Not delivered: the item was not unlocked in your account and we could not resolve this within 3 working days of your request;
  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same item (the extra payment is refunded);
  • Failed payment: money was debited but the purchase shows as failed and the amount is not reversed automatically within 7 working days;
  • Not as described: the item or price differs materially from what was shown before payment.

Requests made after 7 days will still be reviewed fairly.

2. Not refundable

Digital items that were delivered correctly and work as described are not refundable, including where you change your mind. Where an offer shows, before payment, that it includes items you already own, this is not a ground for a refund. This does not limit your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

3. Cancellation

  • You may cancel at any time before completing payment by closing the payment page. Nothing is charged.
  • Our purchases are one-time payments with no recurring charges. A completed purchase cannot be cancelled, but Section 1 continues to apply.
  • If we offer subscriptions in future, you may cancel them at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

4. How to request a refund

Email installngo.official@gmail.com with the subject "Refund" and include the email address of your account, the item, the payment date and, if available, the payment reference (beginning pay_) from your receipt. We respond within 2 working days.

5. Processing

Approved refunds are made in full to the original payment method through Razorpay and normally reach you within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank. Refunded items are removed from your account.
