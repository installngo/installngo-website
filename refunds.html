Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

We want you to be happy with what you buy. If you are not, you can get your money back.

1. 14-day refunds

You can ask for a full refund of any charm pack within 14 days of purchase, for any reason.

2. How to ask for a refund

  • Reply to your receipt email from Paddle, or go to paddle.net and look up your order; or
  • Email us at installngo.official@gmail.com with your order number (it starts with "txn_").

Refunds are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record, and go back to the payment method you used. Banks can take 5–10 working days to show the refund.

3. After a refund

The refunded pack locks again in Danglio on every computer it was used on. If a refunded charm is hanging on your screen, Danglio switches back to a free charm.

4. After 14 days

After 14 days, refunds are at our discretion, for example if a pack does not work as described and we cannot fix it. Your rights under the consumer protection laws of your country always apply.

5. Contact

Questions about refunds: installngo.official@gmail.com.
